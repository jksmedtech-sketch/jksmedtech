What is Docademy

Transforming Fragmented Medical Education
Into a Unified Digital Experience

Medical education in India is at a crossroads. Curriculum delivery is fragmented across lecture halls, photocopied notes, WhatsApp groups, and scattered online resources. Faculty lack tools to create structured digital content. Students lack a single place to learn, practice, and track their progress. Hospitals struggle to ensure CME compliance for their staff.

Docademy replaces this chaotic ecosystem with a single, elegant learning management system purpose-built for medical education. From structured CME courses and interactive clinical case simulations to competency-based assessments and live HD classes — everything your institution needs is unified, trackable, and accessible from any device.

Whether you’re a medical college delivering NMC-aligned curricula, a hospital running CPD programs, or an individual practitioner upskilling for board exams — Docademy makes medical learning measurable, engaging, and effective.
