What is DigiDoc

One Platform. Every Department.
Zero Fragmentation.

Indian hospitals deserve better than duct-taped software. Most facilities run on 5–7 disconnected tools — one for billing, another for EMR, yet another for pharmacy, and a spreadsheet holding everything together with prayers. The result? Data silos, billing errors, compliance nightmares, and staff drowning in manual work.

DigiDoc replaces that entire fragmented ecosystem with a single, beautifully designed cloud platform. From the moment a patient walks in for an OPD appointment to their final discharge summary, every workflow is connected, automated, and auditable. No more re-entering data. No more lost lab reports. No more billing discrepancies at month-end.

Built ground-up for the Indian healthcare ecosystem, DigiDoc is ABDM-compliant, GST-ready, and works in Hindi + English — because real hospital software has to work for real Indian hospitals.
